# Preface

Automation tools are often taught as collections of buttons. That approach can produce a
demonstration, but it does not produce a dependable system.

This handbook treats workflow automation as systems engineering. The reader will learn n8n, but
the durable subjects are events, data contracts, retries, state, authentication, concurrency,
observability, and failure recovery.

The goal is to know when simplicity is safe, when an abstraction leaks, and what evidence to
collect when the system behaves differently than expected.
